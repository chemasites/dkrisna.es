## Choose your nail service

Book manicures, pedicures, and brow and lash treatments at D’Krisna in Caravaca de la Cruz, Murcia, Spain. The menu above lists the price and duration of each appointment.

### Manicures, extensions and maintenance

Explore traditional polish, semi-permanent polish with dipping, acrylic nails, softgel and polygel. Acrylic and gel fills, removal and single-nail repairs are separate appointments. If you already have a nail treatment, ask which service to book so there is enough time for your visit.

### Pedicures, brows and lashes

Dry and spa pedicures are available with traditional or semi-permanent polish. The brow and lash section lists the available waxing, lifting, extension and removal appointments.

## Before booking

### Where can I check prices and availability?

Each treatment has its own booking button. It opens that service in Booksy, where you can check available times and confirm your appointment details.

### When should I book maintenance?

Ask the team about maintenance for your chosen technique and the condition of your nails. If you need removal or a fill, choose the corresponding service or contact us before booking.
