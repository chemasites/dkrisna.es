## Find your appointment in Caravaca de la Cruz

D’Krisna offers massages, facial treatments and wood therapy in Caravaca de la Cruz, Murcia, Spain. Check each service’s name, duration and price before booking.

### Relaxation, hot stone and therapeutic massages

Choose a relaxation massage, a geothermal hot stone massage or a therapeutic massage session. These are separate services with individual bookings. If you are unsure which to choose, ask the practitioner about the session that fits what you are looking for.

### Facial treatments and wood therapy

Facial treatments and wood therapy have separate sections in the menu. Packages specify the number of sessions included; read the description and conditions in Booksy before choosing one.

## Before booking

### How do I book a specific massage?

Select the booking button for your chosen session. Booksy opens with that service selected so you can check availability and continue with your appointment.

### Where can I check the price and duration?

Both appear beside each treatment’s name in the menu. Review the details in Booksy when confirming your appointment too.

Yes. {% whatsapp_link() %}Message us on WhatsApp{% end %} to describe what you are looking for or ask about the session. You can also [meet the team](@/sobre-nosotros.en.md) and find [the salon’s location](@/ubicacion.en.md).
